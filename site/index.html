Aside

A quiet personal assistant that plans tomorrow for you.

Aside is a personal project. It keeps track of tasks, notes and daily activity, and every evening it drafts the next day's schedule as time-boxed blocks in Google Calendar. It is built for one person's own use and is not offered to the public.

What it does

CapturesNotes, journal entries and tasks from a laptop and a phone, with no friction.
PlansDrafts tomorrow's schedule each evening, placing tasks in sensible time blocks.
SyncsKeeps the schedule in Google Calendar and tasks in Google Tasks, so both can be edited anywhere.
ReviewsCompares what was planned with what happened and notices patterns over time.

Principles

  • Never in the way. No nagging; everything is visible, editable and undoable.
  • The calendar is yours. A change you make in Google Calendar is treated as the plan.
  • Your data stays yours. It lives in a private database and is used only to run the assistant.

Google account access

When the owner signs in with Google, Aside requests access to Google Calendar and Google Tasks only, to show and synchronise the owner's own schedule and tasks. See the privacy policy for details on what is accessed, how it is used and how to revoke it.
